New drum, old paper, a print that went wrong in a good way.

Most of the work happens before the drum ever turns: separating the drawing into layers, deciding which colour carries the shadows, and accepting that registration will drift by a millimetre or two.

## What I learned

- Fewer colours, more decisions.
- Paper matters more than ink.
- The misprints are often the keepers.
