Noor Vermeulen is an illustrator and printmaker working from a shared studio on the Rotterdam waterfront.

She draws for magazines, publishers and the occasional ferry company, and prints small editions on a 1998 Riso MZ970.

## Clients

- The Harbour Quarterly
- Uitgeverij Kade
- Northline Ferries

> "A print is a drawing that learned to repeat itself."
